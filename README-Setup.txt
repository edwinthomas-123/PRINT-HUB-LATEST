PRINTHUB LOCAL PRINT BRIDGE - QUICK SETUP GUIDE (30 SECONDS)
====================================================================

Congratulations! This package connects your computer and physical printers
directly to your PrintHub Cloud Store.

SETUP INSTRUCTIONS:

FOR WINDOWS (Recommended - 99% of Print Shops):
1. Extract all files from this ZIP into any folder on your computer
   (e.g., Desktop or Documents).
2. Double-click "Start-PrintBridge.bat"
3. That's it! 
   - A window will open showing "PRINT BRIDGE IS ACTIVE".
   - It runs natively without installing any software.
   - When a customer orders or pays, documents print automatically!
   - Keep that window open (or minimized) during business hours.

FOR MACOS:
1. Double-click "Start-PrintBridge-Mac.command"
   (If prompted by Gatekeeper, right-click and choose "Open").

AUTO-DETECTION & TESTING:
1. Open your PrintHub Shop Dashboard in your browser.
2. The indicator will show green: "● Print Bridge Connected".
3. Click "Test Print" to verify your printer feeds a test sheet.

Need help? Contact support or visit your PrintHub dashboard.
